02 — DESIGN THAT HOLDS

Four good instincts.
One calmer on-call.

Every guarantee has a cost. Name the tradeoff up front, let policy choose it, and make degraded behavior legible.

01⌁

Spread the risk

Hash objects to stable placement groups, then place replicas across hosts or zones. A rack failure should not take every copy with it.

PLACEMENT · CRUSH / CONSISTENT HASHING
02≋

Choose your quorum

For each object, tune R, W and replica count N. R + W > N makes read/write sets overlap; a consensus metadata plane gives ordered map changes.

CONSISTENCY · EXPLICIT TRADEOFF
03◉

Trust the checksum

Carry a content hash with each version. Verify on read and scrub in the background; repair from a matching, authoritative copy.

INTEGRITY · DETECT BEFORE REPAIR
04⤴

Recover with restraint

Throttle backfill, prioritize under-replicated hot data, and keep client traffic moving. Erasure coding lowers space cost for colder, larger objects.

RECOVERY · BALANCE SPEED & I/O

etcd & Raft
